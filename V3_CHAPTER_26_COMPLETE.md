5. **Apathy Versus Petulance** - Giving up vs. seeking attention

### **Power of "The Fearful Glance"**

The entire chapter centers on one perfect gesture: Linton "glanced fearfully towards the Heights." This single detail reveals:
- Who controls him (Heathcliff, not Cathy)
- Why he keeps her there (orders, not desire)
- His constant state of terror
- The source of real power despite physical absence

One gesture > pages of explanation. Perfect teaching moment!

### **The Compassion Trap Analysis**

Deep exploration of how Cathy's genuine compassion for Linton's real suffering actually serves Heathcliff's revenge. The worse Linton looks, the harder for Cathy to leave. Weaponized suffering—disturbing and brilliant.
