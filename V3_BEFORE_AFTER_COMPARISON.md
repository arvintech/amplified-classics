## 🎯 DEPTH COMPARISON

### Key Quotes: BEFORE vs AFTER

#### ❌ BEFORE (Variable Quality):
```typescript
quotes: [
  {
    text: "He always contrived to convey the impression that 
    Catherine's society was necessary to him",
    analysis: "This shows Linton manipulating Cathy."
  }
]
```
**Issues:**
- Too brief (no depth)
- States obvious (doesn't teach)
- No modern connection
- Doesn't model literary thinking

#### ✅ AFTER (V3 Standard):
```typescript
quotes: [
  {
    text: "He always contrived to convey the impression that 
    Catherine's society was necessary to him, that she must 
    come, or he would die, and yet he would deliberately 
    misuse her when she arrived.",
    
    analysis: "This perfectly captures manipulative relationship 
    dynamics: create dependency through neediness, then punish 
    the person who responds. Linton's pattern—'you must come 
    or I'll die' followed by cruelty—is emotional abuse 101. 
    Modern readers recognize this in toxic relationships where 
    people claim they'll harm themselves without you, then 
    treat you terribly when you stay. Brontë identifies 
    psychological manipulation decades before it had a name."
  }
]
```
**Improvements:**
- 50-75 words (speed-readable)
- Identifies pattern (teach thinking)
- Modern parallel (2025 relevance)
- Literary credit (Brontë's insight)
- Feels fast, delivers depth

```

🔹 Sheltered to Vulnerable (monitoring creates lack of judgment)

```

**Result:** Respects Victorian context AND makes 2025 relevant
